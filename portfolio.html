Log #42: Oxygen Systems in Project: Null

October 15, 2026 | By Lead Programmer

This week we completely overhauled the survival mechanics for Project: Null. Previously, the oxygen depletion was strictly time-based. We've implemented a dynamic system where sprinting, taking damage, or being near a hull breach drastically increases O2 consumption.

Log #41: Aethelgard's Combat Flow

September 28, 2026 | By Combat Designer

We've dialed back the animation lock on heavy attacks in Chronicles of Aethelgard. Player feedback from the internal Alpha showed that the game felt too sluggish against agile bosses. Adding a dodge-cancel window at frame 14 has made combat 60% more fluid.
